# Evolith Commercial Licensing

Evolith is developed as an open-source project with a commercial licensing path.

## Open-source license

Unless a file or component states otherwise, Evolith is available under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only (`AGPL-3.0-only`)**. See [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE).

The AGPL license permits commercial use, modification, distribution, and operation of Evolith, subject to its terms. In particular, operators of modified versions that interact with users over a network may have source-code obligations under section 13 of the AGPL.

## Commercial license

The Evolith project owner may offer a separate commercial license for organizations that want to use Evolith under terms different from the AGPL. Typical cases may include:

- incorporating Evolith into proprietary or closed-source products;
- distributing modified Evolith builds without applying the AGPL to the resulting covered work;
- operating materially modified hosted versions without relying on the AGPL licensing path;
- obtaining contractual terms for enterprise deployment, support, warranty, indemnity, or other commercial requirements.

Commercial licensing does **not** revoke or reduce the rights already granted under the AGPL. It is an alternative licensing path for parties that need different terms.

Commercial terms are negotiated separately and are not granted by this document.

## Evolith Cloud

The project owner may provide an official managed service under the Evolith or Evolith Cloud name.

A subscription to an official hosted service is a service agreement; it does not, by itself, grant a commercial software license beyond rights explicitly stated in the applicable service terms.

Nothing in the AGPL prevents third parties from providing commercial services based on Evolith when they comply with the AGPL and other applicable rights. The commercial differentiation of the official service may include operations, hosting, support, integrations, enterprise features, service levels, and other separately provided capabilities.

## Trademarks and branding

Software licensing and trademark rights are separate. The AGPL does not grant permission to use the Evolith name, logos, or branding in a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or that a third-party service is the official Evolith service.

Referential use such as “powered by Evolith” or “compatible with Evolith” should be accurate and should not imply official status.

## Third-party components

Evolith includes and depends on third-party software. Those components remain subject to their own licenses and notices. A commercial license for Evolith does not replace third-party license obligations.

## Contributions and relicensing

Maintaining a commercial licensing path requires the project owner to retain sufficient rights to accepted contributions. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for the current contribution policy.

Until a formal Contributor License Agreement (CLA) process is in place, the project will not merge substantive third-party code contributions that could prevent future commercial relicensing.
